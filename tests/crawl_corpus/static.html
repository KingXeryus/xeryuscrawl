The Oud

The oud is a short-neck, fretless lute played across the Arab world, Turkey, Iran and Central Asia.

A modern Arabic oud usually has eleven strings: five pairs and a single bass string.

Tuning

A common Arabic tuning, from high to low, is C, G, D, A, F and C.

CourseNote
1C4
2G3

Because the neck has no frets, players can slide into the quarter tones that maqam music needs.
